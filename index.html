Meesho GST Tool

GSTR-1 JSON from your Meesho reports. Files stay on your computer.

1 Add files

From the Meesho GST report: tcs_sales.xlsx and tcs_sales_return.xlsx. From Meesho tax invoices: Tax_invoice_details.xlsx. Add last month's Tax_invoice_details too, so credit notes for older orders are found.

    3 Checks

    4 Table 13: documents issued

    Invoice and credit-note number ranges, filled from Tax_invoice_details. Check them, edit any cell, or add a row. These rows go into the JSON. If you fill Table 13 on the portal by hand, type them exactly like this.

    Nature of documentSr. No. fromSr. No. to TotalCancelledNet issuedSource

    5 Review and download

    Upload on the GST portal: Returns Dashboard → GSTR-1 → Prepare Offline → Upload. Then check the summary on the portal before you file.

    6 GSTR-3B: check and pay

    File GSTR-1 first. The portal then fills GSTR-3B Tables 3.1 and 3.2 from it; these are the figures you should see there. No ITC is included (Table 4 = 0).

    Payment

    Type the balance in your Electronic Cash Ledger (portal: Services → Ledgers), after accepting Meesho's TCS in "TDS and TCS credit received". Leave 0 if there is none.

    Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. You remain responsible for checking the return before filing.
